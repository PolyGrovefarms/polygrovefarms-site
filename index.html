🌿 Our Rainforest Homestead

Nestled in the lush uplands of Fern Forest at over 2,200 feet elevation, Polygrove Farms operates as an integrated, off-grid agricultural system. We harness natural tropical rhythms, 48V solar power, and Korean Natural Farming (KNF) principles to cultivate nutrient-dense food, raise heritage poultry breeds on clean unmedicated feeds, and steward wild honeybee colonies.

🐣 Pastured Poultry & Eggs
