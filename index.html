🌿 Our Story & Homestead Mission

Nestled on the lush slopes of the Big Island in Hawaii, Polygrove Farms is an integrated homestead and farm dedicated to sustainable agriculture, ethical animal husbandry, and thriving apiaries.

Our pasture-raised flock enjoys fresh tropical air, natural foraging, and exceptional care—producing rich chicken and duck eggs daily. Alongside our poultry, we tend healthy honeybee colonies to craft raw island honey, hand-poured beeswax candles, and natural skincare. We are passionate about supporting our local community through sustainable farming and humane bee removal services.
