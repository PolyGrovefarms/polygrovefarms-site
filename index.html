Welcome to Polygrove Farms

Operating an integrated, off-grid homestead and farm at 2,500 feet elevation. We focus on unmedicated organic care, natural pasture management, and Korean Natural Farming (KNF) methods to cultivate a thriving ecosystem tailored to our rainforest climate.

Our Poultry Roster

Healthy, vibrant heritage and production lines raised on natural pasture

Waterfowl

Active foragers with distinct upright posture and high egg-laying capacity.

  • Runner Ducks: Upright, penguin-like stride with a 28-day hatching timeline.

Chicken Breeds

Hardy layers and robust dual-purpose birds providing a colorful egg basket.

  • Rhode Island Reds: Deep rust-red plumage, steady and dependable.
  • Jersey Giants: Massive, heavy-bodied birds with commanding presence.
  • Blue Andalusians: Striking slate-blue feathers with dark lacing.
  • Red Stars (Sex Links): Golden-red layers with friendly dispositions.

Turkeys

Magnificent heritage and broad-breasted lines managed with care.

  • Bourbon Red Turkeys: Rich chestnut-mahogany plumage with clean white flight feathers.
  • Broad Breasted White Turkeys: Heavy-set commercial meat lines with solid white frames.
