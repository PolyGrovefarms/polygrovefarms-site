🌿 Our Story & Mission

Nestled on the lush slopes of the Big Island in Hawaii, Polygrove Farms is an integrated homestead and farm dedicated to sustainable agriculture, ethical animal husbandry, and thriving apiaries.

Our pasture-raised poultry flock enjoys fresh tropical air, natural foraging, and top-tier care. Beyond our fresh eating and duck eggs, we manage honeybee colonies to produce raw, small-batch island honey, handcrafted beeswax candles, and nourishing skincare. We are passionate about supporting our local community through sustainable farming and humane bee removal services.
